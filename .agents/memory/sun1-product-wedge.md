---
name: SUN 1 product wedge
description: Research-backed positioning and validation decision for SUN 1's first customers.
---

SUN 1 should initially be positioned as an authorization and evidence operating system for solo authorized researchers and small security consultancies, not as a general AI security scanner for developers. Its defensible promise is explicit scope, bounded review, reproducible evidence, drift, retest history, and report-ready records.

**Why:** Existing tools already cover broad SAST, SCA, DAST, interactive web testing, and asset discovery. SUN 1's current implementation has a real workflow wedge but no proven detector or coverage advantage, and its developer workflow lacks CLI/CI/SARIF/baseline contracts.

**How to apply:** Validate with an 8–12 user, 30-day concierge pilot before adding broad analyzers or marketing to the mass developer market. Measure repeat reviews, evidence-to-report time, accepted findings, duplicates, and willingness to keep using it.
